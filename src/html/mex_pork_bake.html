Mexican-style Pork Pasta Bake
Ingredients
    250g GF Penne
    2 tsp Olive Oil
    400g pork mince
    500g Leggo's Napoletana sauce with chunky tomato & herbs
    1½ tsp Chipotle seasoning
    500g Frozen carrots, peas & corn
    ¼ bunch of Coriander, leaves picked

Instructions
  1. Bring a large saucepan of water to the boil. Cook pasta as per directions until tender.
  2. Heat oil in large frying pan, cook mince for 5 minutes.
  3. Stir in sauce and simmer for 5 minutes.
  4. Preheat oven grill 150°C.
  5. Transfer mixture to a large baking dish and grill for 10 minutes.
  6. Serve pasta bake topped with coriander.
