Slow Cooked Massaman Beef Curry
Ingredients
    1 to 1.3kg Chuck beef, no need to cut
    2 tbsp Olive oil
    1 Massaman beef curry paste
    400ml Coconut milk
    1 cup Chicken stock
    8 small potatos, cut into 2cm chunks
    2 Onions cut into wedges

Instructions
  1. Heat the oil in frying pan over medium heat.
  2. Add beef cook for 3-4 minutes per side and transfer to slow cooker.
  3. In small bowl combine curry paste, coconut milk, and chicken stock.
  4. In slowcooker layer beef, potatos, and onions, pour sauce over the top.
  5. Cover and cook on low for 7-8 hours.
  6. At the end of cooking pull the beef apart to make bite-sized pieces.
  7. Server over rice (optional) with coriander and crushed peanuts.
