Orecchiette w/ Sausage & Brocolli
Ingredients
    450g of Gluten free pasta
    5 tbsp Olive oil
    3 Garlic cloves minced
    450g Sausage mince
    1 cup Chicken stock
    450g Brocolli florets
    ½ tsp Salt
    3 tbsp Unsalted butter
    ½ cup grated pecorino Romano

Instructions
  1. Cook pasta according to package instructions.
  2. Heat 1 tbsp oil in pan, crumble sausage and cook for 5-6 mins.
  3. Add garlic and cook for 1 minute more.
  4. Add remaining Olive oil, chicken stock, brocolli, and salt cook on medium heat 3-4 minutes.
  5. Stir in butter and melt, simmer for a few mins to concentrate sauce.
  6. Drain pasta, add sausage and brocolli mixture, with ½ the cheese and toss to mix.
  7. Serve and sprinkle with remaining cheese.
