Texas Pulled Pork
Ingredients
    1 tsp Olive Oil
    1.6kg Pork shoulder roast
    1 cup BBQ sauce
    ½ cup Apple cider vinegar
    ½ cup Chicken stock
    1/4 cup Brown sugar
    1 tbs Yellow mustard
    1 tbs Worcestershire sauce
    ½ tsp Chili powerder
    1 extra large Onion
    2 large Garlic cloves minced
    1½ tsp dried Thyme

Instructions
  1. Pour olive oil into bottow of slow cooker.
  2. Place pork roast in slow cooker.
  3. Stir in brown sugar, yellow mustard, Worcestershire sauce, chili powder, onion, garlic, and thyme.
  4. Cook on low for 10-12 hours.
  5. Remove pork and shred, return and stir to combine with juices.
