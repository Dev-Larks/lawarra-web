Tuscan Garlic Chicken Thighs
Ingredients
    6 Chicken thigh fillets
    6 cloves Garlic
    1 cup thickened Cream
    ⅓ cup (80ml) Chicken stock
    ¾ cup Parmesan cheese
    1 tablespoon Italian seasoning
    Sea salt and fresh cracked black pepper
    ½ cup Sun-dried tomatoes (chopped)
    2 cups Spinach (chopped, packed)

Instructions
  1. Heat oil or butter in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Add garlic and saute for about a minute until fragrant.
  2. Add the cream and chicken broth. Bring to a gentle simmer, then set the heat to low and simmer for about 10 minutes. The sauce should thicken enough to coat the back of a spoon.
  3. Meanwhile, place the chicken thighs at the bottom of the slow cooker.
  4. Season chicken thighs lightly with Italian seasoning, sea salt, and black pepper.
  5. Top chicken thighs with sun-dried tomatoes.
  6. When the cream sauce is ready, lower the heat and whisk in the Parmesan cheese, little by little, until smooth.
  7. Pour the cream sauce evenly over the chicken thighs, try to get as much sauce as possible on top of the chicken thighs.
  8. Cook your crockpot Tuscan Garlic Chicken thighs for 3-4 hours on HIGH or 6-8 hours on LOW.
  9. When cooking time is over, gently remove the chicken thighs from the crockpot and set it aside.
  10. Turn the slow cooker to HIGH, add the chopped spinach to the crockpot and stir for a few minutes.
  11. Return the chicken to the slow cooker, and spoon the liquid, spinach, and sun-dried tomatoes on top, or just pour them over the Tuscan chicken when serving.
