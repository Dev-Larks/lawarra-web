Chicken Taco Bowlsajitas
Ingredients
    1 kg Chicken breast
    2 packets Taco seasoning
    1 can Black Beans
    340g frozen Corn kernels
    440g Salsa
    ½ cup Chicken stock
    For serving: Tortillas, Sour Cream, Avocado, Tomatoes, Guacamole, Cheese

Instructions
  1. Add corn, black beans and salsa to your slow cooker..
  2. Add the chicken in a nice, even layer over the other ingredients.
  3. Top with taco seasoning.
  4. Pour the water or broth over the chicken.
  5. Cook on high for 3 to 4 hours or on low for 6 to 8 hours.
  6. Serve hot with your choice of toppings:
      7. Grated cheese, sliced avocado, sour cream, tomatoes etc.
