Chia Coconut Bar
Ingredients
    160g Medjool dates (pitted)
    150g Desiccated coconut
    175g Blanched almonds
    60g Macadamias
    60g Walnuts
    2 tbs Pumpkin seeds
    1 tbs Chia seeds
    1 tbs Wheatgrass powder
    ½ tsp Vanilla extract
    ½ tsp Matcha powder

Instructions
  1. Put all ingredients in a food processor and mix until they form a dough.
  2. Line a tray, press mixture in, smoothing over surface.
  3. Sprinkle on coconut.
  4. Freeze, cut into squares and store in fridge
