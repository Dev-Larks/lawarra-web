Honey Garlic Chicken with Potatos and Green beans
Ingredients
    ⅓ cup Honey
    ⅓ cup Soy sauce
    3 tbs Tomato sauce
    2 Garlic cloves, minced
    1 kg Chicken thighs or breast
    ½ tsp Salt
    500g Potatos, sliced in half
    250g Green beans

Instructions
  1. Make glaze combining honey, soy,tomato saurce, and garlic. Set aside.
  2. Season chicken with salt and pepper.
  3. In slow cooker, add chicken, potatos. Pour glaze on top.
  4. Cook on low for 6-8 hours.
  5. 30 minutes before you are ready to eat add green beans.
