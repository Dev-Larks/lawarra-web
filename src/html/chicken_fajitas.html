Chicken Fajitas
Ingredients
    1 Onion, diced or thinly sliced
    3 Chicken breasts
    3 Capsicums (Yellow, Green, Red)
    ½ Lime
Fajita Seasoning
    ⅛ teaspoon Chilli powder (mild)
    ½ teaspoon Paprika
    1 teaspoon Garlic powder
    ½ teaspoon Oregano
    ½ teaspoon Salt
    ½ teaspoon Black Pepper
    For serving: Tortillas, Sour Cream, Avocado

Instructions
  1. Add fajita seasoning ingredients to a small bowl and combine.
  2. Generously sprinkle seasoning on both sides of chicken and use fingers to press it into chicken.
  3. Heat oil over medium heat, sear breasts for 7-8 minutes on each side.
  4. Once chicken has finished cooking move to a plate and let it rest for a few minutes.
  5. Add the onion and capsium and saute for 4-5 minutes stirring frequently.
  6. Slice the chicken breast into strips and add it back to the pan, squeeze lime juice and combine.
  7. Serve immediately.
  8. Serve hot with your choice of toppings:
      9. Grated cheese, sliced avocado, sour cream, etc.
